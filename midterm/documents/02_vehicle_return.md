# ZaenDrive vehicle use and return guide

Fictional coursework rules, version 1.0, 31 October 2026. This guide extends the sample CSV information with return procedures. It is a document source for the retrieval system.

## Fuel and battery charging on return

Petrol and diesel vehicles must be returned with a full fuel tank. Electric vehicles must be returned with at least 80 percent battery charge. These requirements also apply when the vehicle is returned earlier than planned. Fuel and charging costs are excluded from the assistant's rental quotes. If a customer expects to miss the fuel or battery requirement, they should contact staff before returning the vehicle. Staff check the tank or battery level during handover. The amount of any refuelling fee, charging fee, service fee or penalty for a shortfall is not specified in the demonstration data. The assistant must not invent one or treat a price estimate as an invoice. Charging speed, connector compatibility, public charger access and the exact range of a specific vehicle are also not given by this guide; do not infer them from the vehicle name.

## Late return and extensions

Contact staff before the agreed return time if a late return is expected. The assistant cannot extend the rental agreement or promise that an extension is available. A new quote for an extra day does not amend an existing rental agreement. A traffic delay, a charging delay or a message in the chat does not automatically create an approved grace period. Exact late return charges and any grace period are not specified in the demo data. The assistant must state that this information is unavailable and refer the customer to staff. It must not use the normal daily rental rate as a late return fee or invent a charge per hour. Staff need to review the existing agreement and the planned return time before confirming any change.

## Vehicle condition and handover records

At pickup, the customer should review the vehicle's condition together with staff and point out any visible damage before leaving the depot. At return, staff perform a handover check covering the vehicle's condition, fuel or battery level, keys and supplied accessories. The chat does not store inspection photographs or create an official damage report. A statement in the chat is not a signed handover record. If the customer disagrees with a condition report, ask them to contact staff with the details of their concern. The assistant cannot decide responsibility, approve a repair, remove a damage charge or declare that an issue is covered by insurance. Repair prices and damage assessment rules are not specified in this demo. Do not turn a general description of a mark or noise into a financial estimate.

## Cleaning and personal belongings

Remove personal belongings and ordinary rubbish before returning the vehicle. Staff inspect the vehicle during handover. The handbook does not specify a cleaning charge, a smoking rule or a smoking penalty. Do not invent permission, a ban, a required cleaning service or a fee for a case that is not documented. Customers who leave an item in a returned vehicle should contact staff with a description of the item and the intended rental dates. The assistant cannot search a lost property database, confirm that an item was found, or arrange shipping. No storage period, postage price or guarantee that an item will be recovered is provided. A staff referral in the app only displays fictional contact details; it does not submit a lost property report.

## Breakdown support

For a vehicle problem, contact staff and describe the vehicle and the issue. The assistant provides the fictional staff contact only and does not dispatch roadside assistance or open a repair ticket. This demo has no roadside assistance contract, service hours beyond the listed staff hours, towing price or guaranteed response time. Do not promise that a repair, replacement car or tow will be free. Do not diagnose a mechanical or electrical fault from the user's description. Staff must review the situation and any assistance arrangement. The assistant cannot approve continued driving, authorize repairs or ask the customer to modify the vehicle. This handbook contains no vehicle specific repair instructions.
