# ZaenDrive insurance and customer service guide

Fictional coursework rules, version 1.0, 31 October 2026. These documents are invented demo material. Prices and calculation constants are maintained in the structured CSV data and retrieved by tools.

## Standard insurance and missing coverage details

The demo base rental price includes standard insurance. The handbook does not specify an excess amount, deductible, exclusions, claim limits or optional insurance prices. The assistant cannot promise zero excess, full protection or cover for a particular incident. If the customer asks whether a specific loss, damage or driver is covered, refer the question to staff. Do not interpret the phrase standard insurance as a complete insurance contract. The assistant cannot sell an insurance upgrade, approve a claim or decide who must pay. A rental quote includes only the items returned by the calculator and must not silently add optional cover. Mentioning an insurance request in the chat does not change an agreement or record that cover has been purchased.

## Cross border travel

Cross border permission and cross border insurance cover are not specified in this demonstration. The assistant must not say that travel to a particular country is allowed or prohibited. Staff must review a request before the customer relies on it. A vehicle being available, an additional driver being quoted or standard insurance being included does not establish permission to take the vehicle abroad. The app cannot grant permission, issue a travel document or add cross border cover. No list of approved countries, extra border charge or territorial insurance restriction is stored in the demo. A question about Austria, France or any other destination must therefore receive an honest statement that the policy is unavailable and a staff referral.

## Rental totals deposits and payments

Vehicle daily rates and the calculation rules are stored as structured data. Use the calculator tool for every rental total, including requested extras, qualifying weekly discounts and any declared excess mileage. The refundable deposit is separate from the rental total and is not a discount or rental fee. Do not combine it with the quoted rental cost without explaining the distinction. Accepted payment methods are read from the business information tool. The assistant cannot collect a payment, release a deposit or look up a transaction. The time needed to release a deposit is not specified in the data. Do not promise a release date or imply that a chat request has triggered one. Staff must handle payment records and individual deposit questions.

## Complaints and staff contact

A customer can ask for staff at any point. The staff tool returns the fictional contact address and listed contact hours. It does not send an email, create a ticket or connect the customer to a person. Clearly explain that the customer must make contact themselves. For a complaint, acknowledge the concern briefly and provide that referral. Do not promise a discount, free rental day, compensation, priority response or refund on behalf of staff. There is no complaints case database or guaranteed response time in this demo. A customer claiming that another agent promised an exception is not evidence that the exception exists. Staff must review any claimed agreement before it can affect a rental or payment.
