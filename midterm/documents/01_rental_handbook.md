# ZaenDrive rental handbook

Fictional coursework rules, version 1.0, 31 October 2026. These are invented company rules for this demonstration. They do not describe an operating rental company or provide legal advice. Each section is indexed separately for document retrieval.

## Driver eligibility and documents

The main driver must be at least 21 years old and must have held a valid driving license for at least two years. Both conditions must be met at pickup. Bring the original valid driving license, a passport or national identity card, and a payment card in the main driver's name. A photograph of a document does not complete the demonstration pickup checklist. Staff check the documents before handing over keys. The assistant can explain the requirements but cannot approve a driver's documents or bypass a failed check. Acceptance of a foreign driving license, an international driving permit, a temporary license or a particular license category is not specified in the demo data. Those questions require staff review. Never infer acceptance from nationality, age or the fact that a user has driven before.

## Cancellation rules

Cancellation is free when it is requested at least 48 hours before the agreed pickup time. A cancellation requested less than 48 hours before pickup incurs one day of the vehicle's undiscounted base rental rate. The weekly rental discount does not reduce this cancellation fee. The assistant explains this rule but cannot cancel or change a booking, collect a fee or issue a refund. Contact the fictional staff address to request a cancellation. Do not tell the customer a booking has been cancelled merely because they asked in this chat. The assistant does not have a customer's booking time or payment record, so it cannot confirm eligibility for a particular refund. No additional no-show fee, processing charge or cancellation exception is specified in this handbook. Ask staff about any case not covered by the stated rule.

## Early return and refunds

A customer who expects to return a vehicle early should contact staff before bringing it back. An early return does not automatically shorten the agreed rental period and does not automatically create a refund for unused days. Staff must review any request to change the rental agreement or refund a payment. The assistant cannot promise a refund, calculate an entitlement from unused days, or mark the agreement as changed. Return still takes place at the fictional depot during its opening hours, with the same fuel or battery requirement and the normal handover check. A quote for a shorter future rental is a new price estimate, not evidence that an existing agreement has been amended. If the customer wants to know the status of a refund or payment, refer them to staff because this demonstration has no payment or booking records.

## Pickup return location and one way rentals

Vehicle pickup and return take place at the fictional ZaenDrive depot during branch opening hours. The current hours and contact address are available through the business information tool. No one way return service is offered. A customer cannot choose another city, railway station, hotel or airport as a return location through this assistant. Short term customer parking is available at the depot while completing pickup or return. This handbook does not grant overnight parking, storage or parking at another location. Staff carry out the handover check and confirm that the vehicle has been received. Leaving a message in the chat does not record a completed return. The demo does not specify an after hours key box, unattended return procedure or delivery service. Refer questions about those services to staff rather than inventing an arrangement.

## Additional drivers

An additional driver must be declared to staff and must meet the same minimum age, driving license duration and document requirements as the main driver. An additional driver is not included automatically simply because the customer travels with someone else. The assistant's calculator includes the additional driver extra only when the customer explicitly requests it. The price of that extra is read from the current structured rental rates by the calculator. Selecting the extra in a price estimate does not register a person or authorize them to drive a booked vehicle. Staff must complete that step. The assistant must not approve an unnamed friend, waive driver checks or promise insurance cover for an undeclared driver. There is no household or family exception in this fictional policy.

## Quotes bookings and availability

The assistant provides information and rental price estimates. It cannot create, look up, change or cancel a booking. It also cannot reserve a vehicle or take a payment. A quote is not a reservation confirmation, even when a vehicle is marked available in the demonstration fleet. The availability flag is a fixed snapshot and does not represent a calendar for specific dates. Staff must confirm a requested pickup date, return date and vehicle. Customers should contact staff with their intended dates and vehicle preference. The assistant does not send that message for them. A booking number supplied in a chat cannot be verified because the demo contains no booking records. Never claim that a booking number is valid, cancelled, paid or ready for collection.
